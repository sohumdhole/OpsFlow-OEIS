# Daily Tier Meeting Board

A 10 to 15 minute stand-up at the start of each shift, run by the team leader in front of the board (physical or screen). The same layout is reproduced as the "Daily Board" page of the OEIS dashboard, so the conversation is about live data, not memory.

## Board layout (SQDCP)

| Column | Question | OEIS source | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S**afety & food safety | Any injuries, near misses, CCP deviations or allergen incidents yesterday? | CCP check records, incident log | Green / red day calendar |
| **Q**uality | What was yesterday's yield and scrap / rework? Any holds or customer complaints? | Batch yield, rework and hold records | Trend vs target |
| **D**elivery | Did we ship every order on time and in full? What is today's plan vs capacity? | Orders, dispatch, production plan | Plan vs actual |
| **C**ost | Labour hours per unit, giveaway, overtime | Labour and batch records | Trend vs target |
| **P**eople & improvement | Who is trained on what? Which improvement ideas are open? | Training matrix, improvement log | Skills matrix, idea count |

## Agenda
1. Safety and food safety first (2 min).
2. Yesterday's performance against target, one line per SQDCP column (4 min).
3. Today's plan, constraints and staffing (3 min).
4. Problems: owner and next step only; problem-solving happens after the meeting (4 min).
5. Close: confirm actions and escalations (1 min).

## Rules
- Start on time, finish on time, stand up.
- Data on the board is updated automatically or before the meeting, never during it.
- Every red item gets an owner and a date, or is escalated to the next tier.
- Escalation path: Tier 1 (line) → Tier 2 (production manager) → Tier 3 (site management), each the same day.
